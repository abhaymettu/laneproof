# Submitting the report (needs Abhay's accounts; nothing has been submitted)

1. Build: `cd paper && tectonic laneproof.tex` (produces laneproof.pdf; results-table.tex comes from `python3 paper/make_table.py`).
2. arXiv: log in at https://arxiv.org/submit, start a new submission, category cs.SE (cross-list cs.AI). New
   submitters to cs.SE may need an endorsement: request one from the endorsement link arXiv shows.
3. Upload `laneproof.tex` and `results-table.tex` (arXiv compiles LaTeX; do not upload only the PDF).
4. Title and abstract: copy from the .tex file. Licence: CC BY 4.0. Comments: "Code and data: https://github.com/abhaymettu/laneproof".
5. Check the compiled preview, then submit. Announcement follows the next arXiv mailing.
